# Scoring Rubric

Use numeric scoring only when requested or useful for a comparison. The supported quality verdict and actual evidence matter more than a total.

## Quality verdict

- PASS: the relevant next-stage quality conditions are met.
- PASS WITH NOTES: usable now with explicit carry-forward constraints.
- REVISE BEFORE NEXT STAGE: concrete defects need focused repair.
- GO BACK A STAGE: the cause belongs to brief, font, layout, asset or motion planning.
- REDO: direction is wrong for the requested role.
- BLOCKED: required evidence is missing, so quality cannot yet be judged.

These describe quality, not permission. Wrong exact text, false data/geometry, unusable alpha, missing required output, or a broken export cannot be offset by a good score elsewhere.

## Optional artifact score: 0-10

| Dimension | Points | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Role, message and content accuracy | 0-3 | Fits the purpose; supplied facts/text are correct; main message is identifiable |
| Structure and readability | 0-3 | Hierarchy, grouping, spacing and viewing-size/time readability |
| Visual direction and craft | 0-2 | Fits selected style; coherent glyph/image/material treatment |
| Production readiness for this stage | 0-2 | Required editable controls, edges, paths, timing or actual output are usable |

Explain deductions with observed issues. Interpret scores within comparable tasks; do not derive PASS automatically from a threshold or compare unlike production stages as equivalent.

## Learning value: separate decision

Record whether there is a reusable finding and why, or none. An excellent one-off asset can receive full quality marks even if nothing needs to be saved. Learning value does not affect production acceptance. Follow learning-capture.md only when reuse is justified and record updates are requested or required.

## Revision order

Fix role or content failures, then structure and readability, then style/craft and polish. Repair the owning layer. Do not keep iterating after requirements and appropriate checks are met merely to raise a subjective score.
